Pawsitively devoted

At Treat&Tail, our love for dogs extends beyond just providing them with nutritious food. We are dedicated to ensuring the well-being and happiness of every furry friend. From crafting our recipes with care to sourcing the finest ingredients, every decision we make is driven by our passion for dogs. Whether it's supporting local shelters, offering personalized nutrition advice, or simply sharing tips for a happy and healthy life with your companion, we're always here to lend a helping paw. Because when dogs thrive, we thrive too.

Our mission

We're dedicated to crafting dog food that celebrates each pet's unique needs and preferences. We want to embody simplicity, providing organic nutrition that empowers dogs to thrive.

It is our goal to make your life easier with simple online ordering and convenient home delivery. With helpful features such as product videos, customer reviews and photos, you can always make an informed decision about the product for your pet.
